Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website.

Storage we use

We use only strictly necessary storage that is required for the website to function. These include:

  • cookieConsent stores your choice about the cookie banner so it does not reappear on every visit.
  • promoDismissed records whether you have closed the first-visit promotion.

These entries are stored in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers.

Analytics

We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies. If we introduce privacy-respecting analytics in the future, we will ask for your consent before enabling it.

Third-party content

This website does not embed third-party content such as maps, videos or social feeds. As a result, no third-party cookies are set by embedded content.

Managing your storage

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings. You can also reset your cookie choice by clearing your browser data for this site.
