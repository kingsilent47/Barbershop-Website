Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Iron & Comb Barbershop ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have.

Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Name, email address and phone number when you book an appointment.
  • Service preferences and barber preferences.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, through strictly necessary cookies.

We do not collect payment information through this website. Payment is taken in person at the shop.

How we use your information

  • To manage and confirm appointments.
  • To contact you about changes to your booking.
  • To respond to enquiries.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Booking information is retained for up to 12 months after your last visit, after which it is deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. You may also object to the processing of your information or request that we stop contacting you. To exercise these rights, email hello@ironandcomb.co.za.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact the Information Officer

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your personal information can be sent to hello@ironandcomb.co.za or by post to 123 Bree Street, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa.

If you are unsatisfied with how we have handled your request, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
